# Hello, Boundless

<!-- DESC SEP -->

Boundless is my personal blog for notes on code, small tools, and everyday reading.

<!-- DESC SEP -->

## What I Want to Write

I have no plans to dress up every experiment as a polished achievement.

If a small tool fixes an annoyance on my machine, I will write down what it fixed. If an essay is only a passing thought, it can stay that way.

Technical posts will leave behind reproducible commands, configs, and the dead ends along the way. Essays will start from something concrete rather than a grand conclusion.

## Why “Boundless”

The name is a quiet reminder that the world is large, and there is always code I have not read, people I have not met, and questions I have not figured out.

Writing those things down is far more interesting than pretending to already have the answers.
